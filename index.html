02 · The NanoDx Tbit™ test

Three signals. One objective read.

Mild TBI is hard to call with a subjective coma score and a CT scan that usually finds nothing. The Tbit test replaces guesswork with a quantitative result.

GFAPCH 01 · ASTROCYTE
Glial fibrillary acidic protein

Leaks into blood when astrocytes are damaged. A clear flag for structural brain injury.

S100BCH 02 · CALCIUM BINDING
S100 calcium binding protein B

Rises after concussion. Rising levels can flag secondary injury before it shows on imaging.

UCHL1CH 03 · NEURONAL
Ubiquitin carboxy terminal hydrolase L1

Concentrated in neurons. Enters the bloodstream as the brain's distress signal.

03 · Platform

Semiconductor precision, built for diagnostics.

Silicon nanowires coated with biomarker specific antibodies read protein binding as a change in electrical resistance.

Nanowire array · ΔR liveReading

  1. Fingerstick sample

    A drop of blood on a single use cartridge. No centrifuge, no prep.

  2. Targeted binding

    Antibodies on each nanowire capture GFAP, S100B and UCHL1.

  3. Electrical readout

    Each binding event shifts resistance, measured in real time.

  4. Result and sync

    A quantitative result in minutes, sent to the NanoDx data portal.

IP position

Exclusive global license from IBM Research

Worldwide rights to IBM's CMOS compatible nanosensor technology for in vitro diagnostics, backed by NanoDx patents and trade secrets.
